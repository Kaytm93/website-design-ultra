# Claims and Proof

## Claim ledger

Create this before drafting:

| Claim | Status | Evidence/source | Allowed wording | Owner/next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product capability | verified / qualified / illustrative / unknown | URL, document, code, user statement | exact, bounded sentence | person or placeholder |

Do not infer a market claim from an implementation detail. “Uses encryption” does not establish “bank-grade,” and a fast local demo does not establish a production performance percentile.

## Evidence ladder

Prefer, in order:

1. product behavior the user can inspect,
2. measured result with method, sample, date, and scope,
3. named customer evidence with permission,
4. documented certification or independent source,
5. bounded qualitative explanation,
6. explicit placeholder pending evidence.

Logos, ratings, press mentions, awards, and testimonials require a source and usage permission. Avoid anonymous quotes that imitate social proof.

## Hierarchy

Use this sequence as a model, not a mandatory section list:

```text
Specific promise
→ how it works
→ concrete proof/demo
→ objection or constraint
→ next action with expectation
```

- Hero: one audience-relevant outcome, one supporting mechanism/proof, one primary action.
- Feature section: capability → user consequence → evidence or demonstration.
- Pricing/checkout: cost, unit, cadence, inclusions, exclusions, renewal/cancellation.
- Trust section: only controls, certifications, policies, and dates that can be verified.

## Qualification

Use bounds that preserve truth:

- “Up to …” only with the limiting conditions.
- “Typically …” only with representative data.
- “Designed to …” for intention, not proven outcome.
- “Can …” only when the product currently supports the path.
- “Illustrative example” for synthetic/demo content.

Replace vague copy such as “seamless,” “revolutionary,” or “elevate” with mechanism, audience, or observable result.

## Handoff

Ship the claim ledger beside copy. A polished sentence does not erase an unresolved `[source needed]`.
