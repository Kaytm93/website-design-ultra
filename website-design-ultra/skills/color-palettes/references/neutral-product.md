# Neutral and Product Palettes

Use one palette. Values are calibrated for the listed default background/action pairing; recalculate contrast after adaptation.

## 1. Linear Mono

```yaml
bg: "#0a0a0a"
surface: "#171717"
border: "#a1a1aa"
text: "#fafafa"
muted: "#a1a1aa"
action: "#10b981"
on-action: "#052e24"
focus: "#10b981"
danger: "#ff8a80"
on-danger: "#000000"
disabled: "#a1a1aa"
```

Use: productivity, developer tools, dark SaaS.
