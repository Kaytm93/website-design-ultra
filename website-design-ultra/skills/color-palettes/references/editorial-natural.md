# Editorial and Natural Palettes

Use one palette. Recalculate contrast when changing values or applying text to non-default surfaces.

`border` is the 3:1 boundary token and is louder than a decorative rule; use
`divider` for lines that only separate.

## 1. Editorial Cream

```yaml
bg: "#f5f0e8"
surface: "#ebe5d8"
border: "#6b5d4f"
divider: "#d8cfbe"
text: "#1a1a1a"
muted: "#6b5d4f"
action: "#7a3b2e"
on-action: "#ffffff"
focus: "#7a3b2e"
danger: "#9f1239"
on-danger: "#ffffff"
disabled: "#6b5d4f"
decorative: ["#9a793f"]
```

Use: magazines, wine, heritage brands.
